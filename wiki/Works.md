## Themes Across His Works

### 🙏 Spiritual Discovery
Damiam's works consistently explore the nature of faith, the presence of God, and what it means to follow Christ in a thoughtful, questioning way.

### 🧠 Belief and Thought
A recurring theme is the relationship between how we think and how we believe. His work challenges the false dichotomy between faith and reason.

### 📖 Biblical Wisdom
His earlier works drew heavily on the lives of biblical figures—Peter, Moses, Thomas, John, James—examining their struggles and growth.

### 🤝 Relational Faith
Faith is not presented as individual achievement, but as part of our relationships with God and others, especially as fathers and sons.

### ✨ Transformation
Under-girding all his work is the conviction that following God is fundamentally transformative—it changes how we think, act, and relate.
