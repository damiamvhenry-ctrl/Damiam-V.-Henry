- **Biblical Figures** – Deep dives into the lives of Peter, Moses, Thomas, and others
- **Fatherhood and Faith** – The spiritual dimensions of being a father
- **Contemplative Practice** – Silence, reflection, and awareness of God
- **Questions Worth Asking** – Spiritual inquiry that deepens understanding
