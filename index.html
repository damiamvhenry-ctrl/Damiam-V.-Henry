01 / About

The student’s journey

Cape Town · South Africa

Born in Bonteheuwel, Cape Town, Damiam V. Henry is an author, poet, podcaster, father, and lifelong student of God’s Spirit.

At the heart of his identity is a willingness to question, reconsider, observe, and learn. His writing explores how beliefs are formed, how thought shapes our relationship with God, and how silence and reflection can create room for transformation.

He writes not only about what he has learned, but about what he is still learning.
